NIVOX CREATOR STUDIO STARTER MANUAL

Before your session, confirm the equipment list with a hub team member. Keep
food and drinks outside the studio. Route cables away from walkways, use the
provided power protection, and do not force connectors.

For recording, set input levels before starting, monitor with headphones, record
a short test, and save working files to your own approved storage. At the end,
stop recording, safely eject storage, return controls to their neutral position,
power down in the posted order, and report faults immediately.

Only trained staff may open, repair, or rewire equipment.
