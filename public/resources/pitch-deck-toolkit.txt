NIVOX STARTUP PITCH DECK QUICK GUIDE

1. Problem: describe one urgent, specific user pain.
2. Solution: show how your product removes that pain.
3. Market: define the reachable audience and supporting evidence.
4. Product: demonstrate the core journey and current progress.
5. Business model: explain who pays, how much, and why.
6. Traction: use verified usage, revenue, pilots, or research.
7. Competition: explain your defensible advantage honestly.
8. Team: connect relevant experience to execution.
9. Roadmap: identify the next measurable milestones.
10. Ask: state the funding, partnership, or support required.

Keep slides visual, cite every external statistic, and never invent traction.
