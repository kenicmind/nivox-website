NIVOX AI & DATA SCIENCE LEARNING PATH

1. Learn Python, notebooks, data types, and version control.
2. Practice cleaning, exploring, and visualizing real datasets.
3. Study probability, sampling, bias, and evaluation metrics.
4. Build baseline models before complex models.
5. Separate training, validation, and test data.
6. Document data sources, consent, limitations, and intended use.
7. Evaluate accuracy, fairness, privacy, security, and failure modes.
8. Deploy with monitoring and a clear rollback plan.

Never upload confidential or personal data to an unapproved service.
